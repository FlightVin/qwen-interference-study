## 4. Differences found
Severity: **numbers** = changes a reported number; **interpretation** = changes what a result means; **wording** = terminology only.
1. **Diagnostic cap (interpretation; numbers in §6 of report v2).** The local-update diagnostic generated update batches and evaluated J_H/J_R/J_U at cap 8,192 (`diag_local.py:121` @4b47386 and @594d408), although R-s101 r64 trained and was evaluated at 12,288. At r64, 34–39% of diagnostic J_H attempts hit the cap (`research/evidence/a15/diag_r64.jsonl`); no-update J_H was 0.606 against the in-run R-s101 r64 J_H 0.738 (n 8, cap 12,288). PROTOCOL §8 and report v2 §6 do not state the cap. ΔJ at r64 measures change under an 8,192 budget.
2. **Two J_H measurements (wording).** In-run J_H = dev subset, n 8, cap of the round; diagnostic J_H = separate evaluation, n 16, cap 8,192, other seeds. Report v2 uses one symbol for both.
3. **Momentum-only steps in training (interpretation).** 18–66 of 512 steps per run had zero gradient yet moved the weights. Report v2 line 37 ("The practice slots therefore mostly cost generation time without updating the model") is too strong: all-correct practice groups add no gradient, but every step still updates the weights (the 2 hard groups of the minibatch, or the stored moments), and those groups still enter the 1/32 scaling (`run.py:302`).
4. **"Same hard prompts" ≠ same attempts or same minibatches (interpretation).** Round-1 shared-slot attempts were token-identical in 0–11/64 between arms. `grpo.py:55` iterates role names in sorted order, so R/U assign shared groups with the second permutation (`aux` < `hard_common`) and H with the first; R and U share grouping, H differs (code reading; not yet checked on data).
5. **H-s202 "r64" names two policies (interpretation).** The stress cell "H-s202 r64" (report v2 line 724) is a2-r60 (checkpoint saved 2026-11-11 03:26Z); the a2-orig round-64 policy (dev 0.658) has no saved weights. Both carry `attempt_id` a2.

7. **Three round conventions (wording; numbers if mixed).** Step rows and training files are 0-based; round rows 1-based; evaluations and checkpoints count completed rounds.
8. **pass@8 has two estimators (wording).** n 32: combinatorial; n 8: observed coverage at 8. Report v2 defines both as "the chance that at least one of 8 attempts is correct".
